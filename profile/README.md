> We're partnering with communities to build a new off-grid AI device for secure, shared, local insights. Our work began in 2017 with [Project Lantern](https://lantern.works), tackling offline-first scenarios for disaster response. We built our first wireless hardware for off-grid communication, then expanded to climate science and learning scenarios. Along the way, we discovered the need for new local technologies for secure data storage, decentralized identity and smarter maps. This repository publishes that work as open source as we evolve into the AI era.

## Vision
Our vision is hyperlocal AI built by and for communities—technology that acknowledges the unique needs and social context of each user. Molded and shaped like clay by those who hold it in their hands. We aim to provide alternatives to traditional data center and cloud solutions, with a focus on ground-up approaches that emphasize privacy and resilience.
